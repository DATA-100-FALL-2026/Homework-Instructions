In 2018, 167 statewide ballot measures were on the ballot in 38 states. Voters approved 116 (70%) and rejected 50 (30%) ballot measures.

Campaigns to support or oppose statewide measures on ballots in 2018 received $1.21 billion ($1,206,623,790) in contributions to support or oppose statewide measures on ballots.
