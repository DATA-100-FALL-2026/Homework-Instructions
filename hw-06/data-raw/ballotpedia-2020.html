In 2020, 129 statewide ballot measures were on the ballot in 34 states. Voters approved 93 (72%) and rejected 36 (28%) ballot measures.

Campaigns to support or oppose statewide measures on ballots in 2020 received $1.24 billion ($1,235,953,406) in contributions to support or oppose statewide measures on ballots.
