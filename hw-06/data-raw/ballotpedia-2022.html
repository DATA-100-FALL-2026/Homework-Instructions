For elections in 2022, 140 statewide ballot measures were certified for the ballot in 38 states. Ballotpedia identified $1.10 billion ($1,102,676,913) in contributions to support or oppose statewide measures on ballots in 2022.

In 2020, for comparison, $1.24 billion was raised through December 31, 2020, to support or oppose 129 statewide ballot measures.
