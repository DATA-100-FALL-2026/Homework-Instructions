Ballotpedia identified $1.33 billion ($1,332,796,857) in contributions to support or oppose statewide measures on ballots in 2024.

In 2022, for comparison, $1.10 billion was raised through December 31, 2022, to support or oppose 140 statewide ballot measures across 38 states.
